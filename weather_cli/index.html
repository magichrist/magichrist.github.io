Weather CLI

Weather forecasts straight from your terminal — written in Rust.

Weather CLI is a fast, dependency-light command-line tool for checking the weather without leaving your terminal. No app, no browser tabs, no clutter — just current conditions and forecasts, rendered quickly with the reliability you expect from Rust.

GitHub

Key Features

  • Current conditions — temperature, conditions, wind, and more
  • Forecasts — short- and long-range outlook from the terminal
  • Rust-powered — fast startup, single binary, minimal footprint
  • Composable — easy to script, pipe, and drop into your shell workflow

Why Terminal-First?

If you live in a terminal, the weather should be a cargo run away — not another widget, another notification, or another tab. Weather CLI keeps it lightweight and scriptable.

Who Is This For?

Developers, sysadmins, and terminal power users who want a fast, reliable weather check baked into their existing workflow.
